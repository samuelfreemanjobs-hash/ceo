## Voice (30-second test)

We sound like:

1. **Direct** — we say what we mean; no filler
2. **Concrete** — specifics over adjectives
3. **Calm** — confident without hype
4. **Practical** — focused on what the client does next
5. **Honest** — we name tradeoffs and gaps in proof

### Words we use

- sprint, clarity, execution, scope, deliverable, working session, roadmap, accountable

### Words we avoid

- synergy, revolutionary, world-class, seamless, unlock, game-changing, best-in-class

### Words we never use

- guaranteed results, risk-free, #1 (without citation), cure/fix (YMYL contexts)

## Proof policy

- Only cite case studies and metrics we can substantiate on request
- Mark unverified claims as "directional" or omit
- Testimonials require written consent on file

---

## Compliance (services business defaults)

### Hard prohibitions (CRITICAL)

- Guaranteed revenue or ROI outcomes
- Implying endorsement by clients without permission
- False urgency ("only 2 spots left") unless true
- Competitor disparagement beyond factual comparison

### Substantiation required

| Claim type | Required |
|------------|----------|
| % improvement | Methodology + sample + date |
| "Trusted by N companies" | Verifiable count |
| Awards | Named award + year |
| Testimonials | Signed release |

### Disclaimers

- Results vary based on client execution and market conditions
- Past performance does not guarantee future results (when citing metrics)
