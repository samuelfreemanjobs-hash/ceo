## 1. Executive summary

Acme Analytics has strong product signals but your GTM messaging still sounds like three different companies depending on who’s pitching. That costs you deals — buyers can’t tell what you’re best at or why they should choose you now.

The **Discovery Sprint** is a fixed two-week engagement. You’ll leave with a validated positioning statement, a one-page offer you can sell against, and a 90-day GTM plan with weekly milestones your team can execute.

**Investment:** $4,500 fixed fee. **Next step:** confirm scope and schedule kickoff (see §9).

---

## 2. Your situation & goals

**What we understand:**
- B2B team (~40 people) with product-market fit signals but unclear positioning in the market
- Sales and marketing using different narratives; pipeline stalls at “we’ll think about it”
- Leadership wants clarity before spending more on ads or headcount

**Why now:**
- You’re preparing for a push into mid-market accounts and need a single story reps can repeat

**Fit:**
- **Good fit:** Teams willing to dedicate 3–5 hours/week during the sprint and involve a decision-maker on the final readout
- **Not a fit:** Pre-revenue exploration or teams expecting us to run paid media or build product

---

## 3. Proposed solution

**Overview:**
In two weeks, leave with a validated positioning statement, a one-page offer, and a 90-day GTM plan you can execute.

**What's included:**
- 4 working sessions (90 min each) over 2 weeks
- Positioning + ICP worksheet (completed collaboratively)
- One-page offer draft (Playbook format)
- 90-day GTM roadmap with weekly milestones
- Async Slack support (business hours, 48h response)

**Out of scope:**
- Paid media setup or creative production
- Legal/compliance review of final external copy
- More than 2 revision rounds on the offer doc
