## 1. Name & one-line promise (honest scope)

- **Offer name:** Discovery Sprint
- **Promise:** In two weeks, leave with a validated positioning statement, a one-page offer, and a 90-day GTM plan you can execute.

---

## 2. For who / not for

**For:**
- B2B founders/operators (10–100 employees) with product-market fit signals but unclear GTM
- Teams willing to dedicate 3–5 hours/week during the sprint

**Not for:**
- Pre-revenue startups still searching for a problem
- Teams wanting us to run ads or build product for them

---

## 3. What's included (bullets, boundaries)

**Included:**
- 4 working sessions (90 min each) over 2 weeks
- Positioning + ICP worksheet (completed collaboratively)
- One-page offer draft (Playbook format)
- 90-day GTM roadmap with weekly milestones
- Async Slack support (business hours, 48h response)

**Out of scope / boundaries:**
- Paid media setup or creative production
- Legal/compliance review of final external copy
- More than 2 revision rounds on the offer doc

---

## 4. How it works (steps + timeline)

| Step | What happens | Timing |
|------|--------------|--------|
| 1 | Kickoff — goals, constraints, access | Day 1 |
| 2 | ICP + positioning workshops | Days 2–5 |
| 3 | Offer draft + roadmap | Days 6–10 |
| 4 | Readout + handoff | Day 14 |

**Client must provide:** stakeholder availability, current metrics (if any), existing sales collateral, decision-maker on readout call

## 6. Pricing (and logic; tiers if any)

**Price / band:** $4,500 fixed fee

**Logic:** Value anchor vs. 4–6 weeks of internal leadership time at opportunity cost; fixed scope limits risk for both sides.

**Primary recommendation:** Single tier — Discovery Sprint at $4,500

---

## 7. Guarantee / terms (only if defensible)

**Guarantee / risk reversal:** If we miss the Day 14 readout due to our delay, we extend one week at no charge.

**Payment & terms:**
- Payment: 50% on signing, 50% before final readout
- Cadence / comms: Slack + weekly async summary
- Boundaries: pauses >2 weeks convert to async-only; scope additions quoted separately

**Compliance flags:** None (general B2B services). Do not claim guaranteed revenue outcomes.

---

## 8. Objection handling (3–5)

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We can do this internally" | You can — this sprint compresses 4–6 weeks and brings an outside lens on positioning blind spots. |
| "Too expensive" | Compare to leadership hours + delayed GTM; fixed fee, fixed scope. |
| "We need ads, not strategy" | Ads amplify a message; without a clear offer they burn budget. We hand off a plan your team or partners can execute. |
