## Problem

Your product works. Your pitch doesn’t — yet.

When sales, marketing, and leadership tell different stories, deals stall. Ads and headcount won’t fix a positioning problem. You need one narrative, one offer, and a plan your team can run next quarter.

## Solution

**Overview:** The Discovery Sprint compresses weeks of internal debate into a structured two-week engagement.

**What's included:**
- 4 working sessions (90 min each)
- Positioning + ICP worksheet
- One-page offer draft
- 90-day GTM roadmap with weekly milestones
- Async Slack support (business hours)

**Out of scope:**
- Paid media setup
- Legal review of final copy
- More than 2 revision rounds on the offer doc
