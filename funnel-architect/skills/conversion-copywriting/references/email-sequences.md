# Email sequence templates

Reference architectures for Funnel Architect Phase 4. Customize voice, proof, and CTAs per brief.

## Welcome (5-email) — post-signup

| # | Timing | Purpose | Subject pattern |
|---|--------|---------|-----------------|
| 1 | Immediate | Confirm + one next step | "You're in — do this first" |
| 2 | Day 1 | Quick win | Specific outcome in 10 min |
| 3 | Day 3 | Social proof + FAQ | Customer result + top objection |
| 4 | Day 5 | Depth feature tied to job | One use case, not tour |
| 5 | Day 7 | Soft CTA / upgrade | Clear ask if trial |

## Activation (5-email) — PLG trial

| # | Timing | Purpose |
|---|--------|---------|
| 1 | Day 0 | Define activation behavior |
| 2 | Day 1 | Team invite / habit trigger |
| 3 | Day 3 | Founder help offer |
| 4 | Day 5 | Proof from similar teams |
| 5 | Day 10 | Trial ending / decision |

## Nurture (7-email) — lead magnet → offer

Educate → agitate → proof → offer. One CTA progression toward demo/purchase.

## Sales (4-email) — BOFU segment

1. Recap problem + fit  
2. Case study (matched ICP)  
3. Objection handling  
4. Deadline or specific ask  

## Re-engagement (3-email)

1. "Still interested?" + value reminder  
2. New proof or feature  
3. Break-up / unsubscribe clarity  

## Abandoned cart (3-email)

1. 1h — reminder + cart link  
2. 24h — objection (shipping, guarantee)  
3. 48h — incentive if brand allows (compliance check)  

## Evaluator checklist (every high-stakes email)

- [ ] Audience is hero  
- [ ] Specific promise  
- [ ] Top objection addressed  
- [ ] One clear CTA  
- [ ] Claims substantiated or flagged  

## Handoff

External sequences → Morgan → `compliance-agent` before send.
