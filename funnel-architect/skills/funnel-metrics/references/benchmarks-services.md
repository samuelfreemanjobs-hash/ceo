# Services funnel benchmarks

High-touch services — pipeline metrics matter more than visit volume.

## Lead gen → close

| Stage | Heuristic | Confidence |
|-------|-----------|------------|
| Lead → qualified | 20–40% | Likely |
| Qualified → discovery booked | 50–70% | Likely |
| Discovery → proposal | 60–80% | Likely |
| Proposal → close | 25–45% | Likely |

## Economics

| Metric | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| CAC | Include sales time + marketing + tools |
| LTV | Project length × margin; watch concentration risk |
| Utilization | Capacity caps growth — flag in funnel model |

## Channel benchmarks

| Channel | Typical CPL | Confidence |
|---------|-------------|------------|
| Referral | Lowest CAC | Confirmed pattern |
| LinkedIn organic + outbound | Medium | Likely |
| Paid search (niche) | High CPC | Varies |
| Webinars | $50–$200/registrant | Likely |

## Cycle time

- Discovery → proposal: 1–2 weeks
- Proposal → decision: 2–6 weeks
- **Funnel math must use weeks, not days**

## Anti-patterns

- Counting "leads" without qualification definition
- Win rate without loss reason tracking
