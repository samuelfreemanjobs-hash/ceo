# Webinars playbook

**ACV sweet spot:** $1K+ · **Cycle:** medium

## When it works

- B2B mid-market+, complex products, courses, high-ticket info
- Education needed before purchase decision
- Paired with paid traffic or owned list

## When it doesn't

- Simple self-serve products
- Low attention audiences without nurture follow-up

## Economics

- 25–40% show rate from registrants
- 5–15% of attendees → meaningful next step
- Best ROI when replay + email sequence captures no-shows

## Creative patterns

- Specific outcome title ("Cut CAC 30% in Q1" not "Product overview")
- Taught content > pitch; live > recorded for engagement
- Strong follow-up sequence for attendees and no-shows

## Measurement

Registrants → attendees → next step → pipeline/revenue

## Top failures

- Pitch slap at end
- Broad topics with weak ICP fit
- No nurture after event
