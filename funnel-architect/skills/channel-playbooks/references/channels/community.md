# Community playbook

## When it works

- Dev tools, prosumer, creator, niche identity products
- Team can show up consistently (not broadcast-only)
- Product benefits from peer learning

## When it doesn't

- Commodity product with no community identity
- No moderator capacity
- Expecting community to replace paid acquisition day 1

## Mechanics

| Tactic | Funnel role |
|--------|-------------|
| Slack/Discord community | Activation, retention |
| Forum / Discourse | SEO + support deflection |
| Events / meetups | Awareness, trust |
| Ambassador program | Advocacy |

## Math

- Hard to attribute directly — use cohort: community members vs non-members (activation, retention, NRR)

## KPIs

| Primary | Guardrail |
|---------|-----------|
| Active members, posts/week | Moderation load |
| Community-attributed signups | Toxicity / churn |

## Failure modes

- Ghost town (no staff participation)
- Community as support ticket dump without value
- Promotional spam allowed
