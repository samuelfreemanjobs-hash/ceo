# Influencer / creator playbook

**ACV sweet spot:** $20+ AOV / $200+ ACV · **Cycle:** short

## When it works

- Consumer products with visual/story angles
- Audiences on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube
- Micro-influencers (10K–100K) often beat mega on ROAS

## When it doesn't

- Most B2B (rare exceptions)
- Scripted brand deals without authentic integration

## Economics

- $0 gifted to $100K+ top creators
- Track unique codes, dedicated LPs, first purchase + LTV

## Creative patterns

- Creator-led content > brand scripts
- Long-term partnerships > one-off posts
- UGC rights for paid amplification later

## Top failures

- Supplying full script (kills authenticity)
- One-and-done partnerships
- No attribution mechanism
