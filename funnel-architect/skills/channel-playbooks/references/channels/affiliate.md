# Affiliate program playbook

**ACV sweet spot:** $50+ AOV / $500+ ACV · Margins must support 20–30% commission

## When it works

- Adjacent audiences via partners who already sell to your ICP
- DTC, SaaS, courses with clear partner profile

## When it doesn't

- Thin margins
- Low-trust products
- No partner manager capacity

## Economics

- Pay per sale — favorable ROI if activated partners exist
- Real cost: recruitment, assets, payouts, support

## Creative patterns

- Partners sell — provide swipe copy, LPs, co-branded assets
- Dead-simple join flow; fast payouts

## Measurement

- Affiliate-attributed revenue
- Partner activation rate (% who drive a sale)
- Partner LTV vs cold CAC

## Top failures

- No assets for partners
- Slow payouts
- Expecting partners to figure it out alone

See also: `partnerships.md` for co-marketing (non-commission).
