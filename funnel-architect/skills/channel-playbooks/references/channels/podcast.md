# Podcast playbook

## Host (own show)

**ACV:** $500+ / brand-driven · **Cycle:** long — not direct response

- Brand building, thought leadership, future audience
- Compounds over years; hard to attribute short-term
- Best paired with email capture and repurposed clips for social

## Guest / sponsor

**ACV:** $100+ AOV / $1K+ ACV · **Cycle:** medium

- Niche communities; B2B role targeting; DTC with story
- Sponsor reads need authentic host fit
- Track with vanity URLs, promo codes, post-purchase survey

## Top failures

- Expecting podcast alone to fill pipeline (B2B)
- Generic sponsor scripts
- No measurement beyond downloads
