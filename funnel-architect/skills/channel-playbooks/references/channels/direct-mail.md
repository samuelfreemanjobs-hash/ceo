# Direct mail playbook

**ACV sweet spot:** $5K+ ACV / luxury DTC · **Cycle:** medium

## When it works

- ABM enterprise (named accounts)
- Luxury DTC, real estate, high-value local services
- Complements digital when mailbox is less crowded

## When it doesn't

- Low ACV volume plays
- Broad untargeted lists

## Economics

- Cost per piece + fulfillment; measure to meeting or purchase
- Best with tight list + personalized trigger

## Creative patterns

- Physical artifact (book, sample, handwritten note)
- Clear QR/URL to dedicated LP
- Multi-touch: mail → email → call

## Top failures

- Generic bulk mail
- No attribution on landing page
- No follow-up sequence
