# TikTok ads playbook

**ACV sweet spot:** $20+ AOV / $200+ ACV · **Cycle:** short · **Audience:** Gen Z + millennial consumer

## When it works

- DTC consumer, lifestyle, creator-friendly products
- Strong UGC/video creative capacity
- Visual product with hook in first 1–2 seconds

## When it doesn't

- Enterprise B2B ($250K ACV)
- Products requiring long explanation
- No video creative pipeline

## Economics

- CPM often lower than Meta; creative quality drives CAC
- Plan learning budget similar to Meta (~$3K–$10K)
- Track MER + platform ROAS; creative refresh every 7–14 days

## Creative patterns

- Native UGC — not polished TV spots
- Problem-led hooks, result-led variants, creator POV
- Spark ads / whitelisted creator content

## Measurement

- Pixel + events; track to purchase/LTV
- Separate campaigns by creative angle

## Top failures

- Repurposing Meta creative without TikTok-native format
- Scaling before creative winners identified
- Ignoring comment sentiment / ad fatigue
