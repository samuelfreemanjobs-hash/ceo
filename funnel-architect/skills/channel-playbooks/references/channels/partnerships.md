# Partnerships playbook

## When it works

- Complementary audiences (not competitors)
- Clear value exchange: leads, co-marketing, integration
- Partner has trust with your ICP

## When it doesn't

- No partner manager capacity
- Vague "let's partner" without economics
- Partner audience mismatch

## Mechanics

| Type | Example |
|------|---------|
| Integration co-marketing | Slack app directory, marketplace listings |
| Referral / affiliate | Rev share, lead pass |
| Co-webinar | Shared list, split nurture |
| Agency channel | Implementation partners |

## Math

- Partner CAC = partner incentives + manager time / customers
- Compare to paid CAC before scaling

## KPIs

| Primary | Guardrail |
|---------|-----------|
| Partner-sourced pipeline | Partner engagement rate |
| Win rate vs other channels | Time to activate partner |

## Failure modes

- Too many small partners (sprawl)
- No tracking (UTM, partner codes)
- Legal/terms delayed forever
