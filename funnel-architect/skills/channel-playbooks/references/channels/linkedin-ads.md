# LinkedIn ads playbook

## When it works

- B2B with defined titles, company size, industry
- Offers: demo, webinar, whitepaper, free trial (team tools)
- ACV supports $80–$200+ CPL

## When it doesn't

- Low ACV consumer products
- Audiences too small (<10K matched)
- Weak BOFU asset (generic homepage)

## Mechanics

| Format | Funnel stage |
|--------|--------------|
| Sponsored content | Awareness, MOFU |
| Lead gen forms | MOFU capture |
| Conversation ads | Event/webinar |
| Retargeting | BOFU |

## Math (heuristics)

| Metric | Range | Confidence |
|--------|-------|------------|
| CPC | $5–$15 | Likely |
| CTR | 0.4–1.2% | Likely |
| CPL | $75–$250 | Likely |

## Creative patterns

- Specific audience call-out in first line
- Stat or outcome-led hook
- Single CTA — no brochure copy

## KPIs

| Primary | Guardrail |
|---------|-----------|
| CPL, SQL rate | Lead quality (MQL→SQL) |
| Cost per demo | Form completion rate |

## Failure modes

- Lead gen forms without nurture sequence
- Targeting too narrow (delivery issues)
- No offline conversion tracking for pipeline
