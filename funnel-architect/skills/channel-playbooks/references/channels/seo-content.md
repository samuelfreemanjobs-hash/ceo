# SEO & content playbook

## When it works

- Compounding channel — 6–18 month horizon
- Searchable problems, comparisons, category terms
- Product can be explained in content

## When it doesn't

- Need revenue in 30 days with zero domain authority
- Category has no search volume
- Product too new for comparison keywords

## Mechanics

| Content type | Funnel stage |
|--------------|--------------|
| Problem/pain posts | TOFU |
| How-to, templates | MOFU |
| vs / alternative pages | BOFU |
| Docs, integrations | Activation (PLG) |

## Math

- SEO ROI = organic sessions × conversion rate × LTV
- Track **non-brand** organic separately from brand

## Creative patterns

- Comparison pages: honest feature table + clear ICP fit
- Programmatic only when genuinely useful (not thin spam)
- Internal links from TOFU → BOFU money pages

## KPIs

| Primary | Guardrail |
|---------|-----------|
| Non-brand organic traffic | Ranking keywords in top 10 |
| Organic-assisted conversions | Bounce rate, time on page |

## Failure modes

- Publishing without BOFU capture (no CTA, no lead magnet)
- Ignoring technical SEO (speed, crawl)
- Chasing volume keywords with zero buyer intent
