# Events playbook

**ACV sweet spot:** $25K+ · **Cycle:** long

## When it works

- Enterprise B2B, high-touch services
- Industry concentration (same city/vertical events)
- Own events for pipeline + brand; sponsor for awareness

## When it doesn't

- Low ACV self-serve
- Early stage without clear ICP booth story

## Economics

- High fixed cost per event — require pipeline targets pre-event
- Track meetings booked, opps created, revenue influenced

## Creative patterns

- Specific demo/narrative for ICP at booth
- Speaking slots > logo sponsorship for credibility
- Aggressive follow-up within 48h

## Top failures

- Swag without conversation capture
- No CRM tagging for event-sourced leads
- Attending wrong conferences (audience mismatch)
