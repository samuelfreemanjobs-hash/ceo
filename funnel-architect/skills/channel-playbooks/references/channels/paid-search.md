# Paid search playbook

## When it works

- Known category, high purchase intent ("buy", "pricing", "vs", "alternative")
- ACV supports CPC economics
- Clear conversion event (signup, demo, purchase)

## When it doesn't

- Pre-category education (use content/social first)
- Low search volume niches
- Long sales cycle without lead capture asset

## Mechanics

| Element | Best practice |
|---------|---------------|
| Structure | Brand, competitor, category, problem keywords |
| Match types | Start exact/phrase; expand with data |
| Landing | Dedicated LP per ad group — not homepage |
| Conversion | One primary CTA per LP |

## Math (heuristics — verify)

| Metric | B2B SaaS | DTC | Confidence |
|--------|----------|-----|------------|
| CPC | $3–$25+ | $0.50–$3 | Likely |
| LP CVR | 3–12% | 2–8% | Likely |
| CPL | $50–$300 | $15–$80 | Varies |

**CAC check:** `CPL / (MQL→customer rate)` or `spend / customers`

## Creative patterns

- Headline mirrors query intent
- Competitor/alternative pages for bottom funnel
- Social proof above fold on BOFU

## KPIs

| Primary | Guardrail |
|---------|-----------|
| CPA / CAC | Quality score, bounce rate |
| Conversion rate | Impression share (brand) |

## Failure modes

- Sending all traffic to homepage
- No negative keywords (budget bleed)
- Tracking conversions without offline pipeline stages
