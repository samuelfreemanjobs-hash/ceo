# Email playbook

## When it works

- Owned list or captured leads
- Lifecycle: welcome, nurture, activation, win-back
- Clear behavioral triggers (signup, cart abandon, inactive)

## When it doesn't

- No list and no capture on site
- Batch-and-blast without segmentation
- Product with no repeat engagement hook

## Mechanics by sequence type

| Sequence | Trigger | Emails | Goal |
|----------|---------|--------|------|
| Welcome | Signup | 3–5 | Orient + first value |
| Activation | Trial start | 5–7 | Drive aha moment |
| Nurture | Lead magnet | 7 | Educate → offer |
| Sales | BOFU segment | 3–4 | Close |
| Re-engagement | 30d inactive | 3 | Revive or clean list |
| Abandoned cart | Cart abandon | 3 | Recover purchase |

## Math

- Email ROI = (revenue attributed) / (tool + creative cost)
- Track: open rate (directional), **click rate**, conversion per send

## Creative patterns

- Subject: specificity + curiosity (not clickbait)
- One CTA per email
- Plain-text founder emails for activation (often outperform HTML)

## KPIs

| Primary | Guardrail |
|---------|-----------|
| Revenue per email / conversion | Unsubscribe rate |
| Click-to-conversion | Spam complaints |

## Failure modes

- No deliverability setup (SPF, DKIM, DMARC)
- Same message to trials and paid users
- Fake urgency / compliance issues → route to compliance-agent
