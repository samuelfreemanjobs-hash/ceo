# Meta ads playbook

## When it works

- B2C, visual product, broad demographic targeting
- Clear offer + fast path to purchase or lead magnet
- Strong creative testing capacity

## When it doesn't

- Niche B2B with tiny audience pools
- Long enterprise sales cycles without nurture
- Products that can't be explained in 3 seconds of video

## Mechanics

| Element | Notes |
|---------|-------|
| Campaign objective | Match to funnel stage (awareness vs conversion) |
| Creative | UGC, demo, problem-solution hooks — refresh every 2–4 weeks |
| Audience | Broad + creative targeting often beats hyper-narrow |
| Pixel/CAPI | Required for optimization |

## Math (heuristics)

| Metric | DTC | Lead gen B2B | Confidence |
|--------|-----|--------------|------------|
| CPM | $8–$25 | $15–$40 | Likely |
| CTR | 0.8–2% | 0.5–1.5% | Likely |
| CPL | $15–$60 | $40–$150 | Varies |

## Creative patterns

- Hook in first 1–2 seconds
- Before/after, unboxing, founder story
- Static: bold claim + single benefit + CTA

## KPIs

| Primary | Guardrail |
|---------|-----------|
| CPA / ROAS | Frequency, CPM creep |
| Thumb-stop rate (video) | Landing page CVR |

## Failure modes

- Optimizing for clicks on cold traffic
- No post-click experience match (ad promise ≠ LP)
- Creative fatigue ignored
