# Outbound sales playbook

## When it works

- High ACV ($15K+ or clear path to it)
- Defined ICP list (firmographics + titles)
- Pain is acute and articulable

## When it doesn't

- Low ACV self-serve ($9–$49/mo)
- No list quality or research capacity
- Product requires product-led trial first

## Mechanics

| Stage | Tactic |
|-------|--------|
| List build | ZoomInfo, Apollo, LinkedIn Sales Nav |
| Touch pattern | Email + LinkedIn + call (multi-channel) |
| Message | Problem → insight → micro-ask (not pitch deck) |
| Handoff | SDR → AE at qualified meeting |

## Math

| Metric | Heuristic | Confidence |
|--------|-----------|------------|
| Reply rate | 1–5% cold | Likely |
| Meeting book rate | 0.5–2% of sends | Likely |
| CAC | Fully loaded SDR+AE cost / wins | Required |

## Creative patterns

- 3-sentence emails, one question
- Personalized first line (real, not mail-merge fluff)
- Case study one-liner matching their segment

## KPIs

| Primary | Guardrail |
|---------|-----------|
| Meetings booked, pipeline $ | Bounce rate, spam reports |
| SQL conversion | Activity per rep |

## Failure modes

- Volume without targeting
- Pitching before discovery
- No CRM hygiene → can't attribute funnel
