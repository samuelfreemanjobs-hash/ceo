# Customer referral program playbook

**ACV:** any · **Cycle:** short · Often highest-leverage underexploited channel

## When it works

- Delighted customers (NPS strong)
- Services, high-touch B2B, products with social proof context

## When it doesn't

- Embarrassing products, one-and-done low-AOV with no story
- Neutral or negative NPS

## Economics

- CAC = incentive ($0–$200) + program ops
- Referred leads typically 3–10× cold conversion; 2–5× better LTV

## Formalization playbook

1. Track referrals by source (12mo lookback)
2. Quantify referred vs cold CAC, close rate, LTV
3. Ask at success moments: post-win, 90d onboarding, renewal
4. Specific ask: "Who else deals with [problem]?" + forwardable intro email
5. Reward **referrer** not just referee
6. CRM tags + monthly referral pipeline report

## Service businesses

Add partner referrals (VCs, accountants, complementary services). 10 active partners → 3–5× ad-hoc referral volume.

## Top failures

- Waiting instead of asking
- No tracking
- Misaligned incentives (only reward new customer)
- Generic "refer us" blast
