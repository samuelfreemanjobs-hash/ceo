# B2C psychographics reference

Use for DTC, consumer apps, info products, and prosumer tools.

## Psychographic dimensions

| Dimension | What to capture |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Identity | How they see themselves ("busy parent", "serious lifter") |
| Values | Sustainability, status, convenience, privacy |
| Lifestyle | Schedule, constraints, media consumption |
| Aspirations | Who they want to become |
| Fears | What they avoid (judgment, waste, complexity) |

## Jobs to be done (consumer)

| Job type | Example prompt |
|----------|----------------|
| Functional | "Help me meal prep in 20 min" |
| Emotional | "Feel in control of my health" |
| Social | "Look like I have it together" |

## Awareness levels (Eugene Schwartz)

| Level | Copy approach |
|-------|---------------|
| Unaware | Problem education, story-led |
| Problem-aware | Agitate pain, introduce category |
| Solution-aware | Differentiate your mechanism |
| Product-aware | Proof, offers, objection handling |
| Most aware | Direct CTA, urgency |

**Funnel stage mapping:** Unaware/Aware = TOFU; Considering = MOFU; Decided = BOFU.

## Trigger events (B2C)

- Life transition (move, baby, job change)
- Seasonal (New Year, summer, back to school)
- Social proof moment (friend recommendation)
- Pain spike (broken product, bad experience)

## Alternatives map (consumer)

| Alternative | When chosen |
|-------------|-------------|
| DIY | Price-sensitive, time-rich |
| Premium incumbent | Trust, habit |
| Cheaper substitute | Good enough |
| Do nothing | Low urgency |

## Voice-of-customer sources

- Amazon reviews (competitor products)
- Reddit, Facebook groups, TikTok comments
- App store reviews
- Survey responses (if user provides)

## Anti-patterns

- Demographics-only personas ("women 25–34")
- Assuming awareness level without evidence
- Copy that leads with features for unaware audiences
