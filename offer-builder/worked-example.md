# Worked example — B2B SaaS reposition

Session trace for Offer Builder. **Input:** agency project tool in crowded PM category.

---

## User brief (summary)

- Product: client project delivery for agencies
- $49/user/mo, 5–25 person boutiques
- Churn: "Asana is good enough"
- No Scout artifact — agent asks for alternatives

---

## Phase 1 — Discovery

**Agent asks:**
1. What triggers a switch from Asana/Monday? (client visibility complaints — confirmed)
2. Who is the buyer vs. daily user? (owner vs. PM)
3. Proof assets? (2 case studies with hours-saved; no ROI guarantee yet)
4. Price ceiling from win/loss? (lost deals at $79, wins at $49)

---

## Phase 2 — Position

**Rejected:** "Project management for agencies" (crowded, no wedge)

**Selected positioning statement:**
> For **boutique creative agencies (5–25 people)** who **lose margin to scope creep and client status questions**, **DeliveryOS** is the **client delivery platform** that **gives clients real-time visibility without exposing internal chaos** unlike **generic PM tools (Asana, Monday)**.

**For / against:**

| For | Not for |
|-----|---------|
| Agency owner, client-facing work | Internal-only tech teams |
| 5–25 seats, $250–1.2K MRR ACV | Enterprise procurement |
| Scope creep pain | Solo freelancers |

---

## Phase 3 — Propose

**Primary value prop:** Stop scope creep and client email chains — clients see delivery status in a branded portal without your team living in inbox triage.

**Proof ladder:**

| Claim | Proof | Status |
|-------|-------|--------|
| Reduces status-update email volume | Case study: 40% fewer client emails | Verified |
| Faster client sign-off | Demo: approval workflow | Likely |
| ROI in 60 days | Not yet substantiated | Unverified → compliance flag |

---

## Phase 4 — Architect

| Layer | Component |
|-------|-----------|
| Core | Platform + client portal |
| Accelerator | Scope templates library |
| Bonus | Agency onboarding workshop (first 10 customers) |
| Risk reversal | 60-day ROI guarantee (conditions TBD — compliance) |

**Tiers:** Starter (3 seats min) / Studio (10 seats) / Agency (25 seats)

---

## Phase 5 — Package

- Anchor: $79/user (competitor mid-tier)
- Sell: $49/user monthly; $39 annual
- 3-seat minimum → ~$147/mo entry

---

## Phase 6 — Validate (ICE)

1. **Category headline A/B** ("Client Delivery Platform" vs "PM for Agencies") — ICE 4.3
2. **Guarantee on pricing page** — ICE 3.7
3. **ICP case study ad** (creative agency only) — ICE 4.0

**Handoff to funnel-architect:** PLG trial funnel, activation = first client invited to portal.
