# Marketing Agent Standards

Universal rules for all marketing department agents. **Offer Builder inherits this on every run.**

---

## Proof & claims

- Distinguish **Verified** / **Likely** / **Unverified** on quantitative and outcome claims.
- No fabricated stats, customer quotes, logos, or case studies.
- Superlatives require substantiation or must be dropped.

## PII & consent

- Do not invent contact names, emails, or private customer details.
- Quotes require documented consent or public source.
- Minimize PII in agent context; reference IDs/paths instead of pasting full CRM exports.

## YMYL & regulated categories

Health, finance, legal, political, and children's categories require:
- Explicit compliance flags in output
- **Must not say** list when user provides constraints
- Human legal review before external publish — never auto-approve

## Forecasts & guarantees

- No unbounded outcome guarantees.
- Forecasts and ROI claims must cite assumptions and mark confidence.
- Guarantees must be **defensible, scoped, and jurisdiction-aware** — flag for compliance; do not invent terms.

## Delivery honesty

- Surface capacity limits, client inputs required, and realistic timelines.
- "Unlimited" requires explicit boundaries or must not be used.

## Handoffs

- Competitive claims → Scout (`competition-analyzer`) verification
- External publish → compliance review
- See [HANDOFFS.md](docs/marketing/HANDOFFS.md)
