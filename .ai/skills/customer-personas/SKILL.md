# Customer Personas — Services GTM

> Sync with `docs/marketing/BRAND-PROFILE.md` ICP section.

## Persona 1: Founder-Operator Fran

- **Title:** Founder or COO, 10–100 person B2B SaaS/services
- **Situation:** Product works; GTM is inconsistent; tired of "we'll think about it"
- **Goals:** One clear story; rep-ready offer; 90-day plan
- **Objections:** "We can do this internally"; price sensitivity
- **Channels:** LinkedIn, warm intro, podcast guest follow-up
- **Offer fit:** Discovery Sprint (primary)

## Persona 2: VP Marketing Victor

- **Title:** VP Marketing, hired to fix positioning
- **Situation:** New in role; inherited fragmented messaging
- **Goals:** Quick win visible to CEO; documented positioning
- **Objections:** Needs buy-in from sales; timeline pressure
- **Offer fit:** Discovery Sprint; may need executive summary proposal

## Anti-personas (do not target)

- Pre-revenue idea stage
- Teams wanting done-for-you paid media only
- Enterprise procurement >90 days
