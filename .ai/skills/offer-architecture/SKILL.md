# Offer Architecture

The offer is **everything the customer gets and believes they're getting** — not just the product. Structure drives perceived value and conversion.

## Prerequisites

- Value prop + proof ladder (know which claims need support)
- Pricing constraints (floor/ceiling if known)
- Delivery capacity (what you can actually fulfill)

## Offer stack layers

| Layer | Purpose | Design rule |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| **Core** | The job they hired you for | One clear deliverable — don't bundle confusion |
| **Accelerator** | Speed or ease (reduces effort/sacrifice) | Onboarding, templates, done-for-you setup |
| **Bonus** | Increases perceived value without core cost | Must relate to outcome — no random swag |
| **Risk reversal** | Increases perceived likelihood | Guarantee, trial, pilot, SLA |
| **Ascension** | Next offer in ladder (note only) | Tripwire → core → premium; don't design full ladder unless asked |

## Tier design (when applicable)

**Good tiering axes:**
- Usage / seats / volume
- Support level
- Feature depth aligned to maturity
- Team vs. individual

**Bad tiering:**
- Artificial crippling that blocks the core job
- More than 4 tiers without clear buyer paths
- "Enterprise: contact us" as only differentiation

## Guarantees & risk reversal

| Type | When to use | Risk |
|------|-------------|------|
| Money-back (30/60/90 day) | Strong product, weak brand awareness | Abuse; set conditions |
| Outcome guarantee | Services, high-touch | Must define measurable outcome |
| Free trial / pilot | SaaS, PLG | Activation must prove value fast |
| SLA / uptime | Infrastructure, B2B | Operational commitment |
| "Keep the bonuses" | Info products | Low marginal cost |

**Always state:** conditions, who qualifies, how to claim.

## Naming

- **Offer name** — outcome or identity ("Client Delivery System" not "Pro Plan Bundle")
- **Tier names** — signal buyer stage or outcome level
- **Bonus names** — specific benefit ("Agency Onboarding Sprint" not "Bonus #2")

## Diagnostic: offer vs. positioning mismatch

| Symptom | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| High traffic, low conversion | Proof or risk reversal weak |
| Long sales cycle, no urgency | Missing accelerator or pilot structure |
| Churn after purchase | Core overpromised; bonus underdelivered |
| Price objections | Stack perceived value or re-tier |

## Anti-patterns

- 12 bonuses that obscure the core offer
- Guarantee you can't operationally honor
- Tier that requires sales call when motion is self-serve
- Bonuses unrelated to stated outcome
- Copying competitor stack without delivery capacity
