# Marketplace funnel patterns

Deep reference for two-sided marketplace GTM funnels. Load when business model = marketplace.

## Two funnels, not one

| Side | Primary job | Typical stages |
|------|-------------|----------------|
| Supply | Attract providers / inventory | Aware → Listed → First transaction → Retained supply |
| Demand | Attract buyers / usage | Aware → First search → First purchase → Repeat |

Optimize the **constraint side** first (usually supply in early marketplaces).

## Chicken-and-egg sequencing

1. **Constrained side first** — often supply (listings, drivers, hosts)
2. **Liquidity metric** — time to match, fill rate, search success
3. **Single geography / category** — nail one wedge before expansion

## Supply funnel signals

- CAC to first listing
- Time to first transaction
- Supply churn / dormancy rate

## Demand funnel signals

- Visit → search → transaction
- Repeat purchase rate
- Cross-side referral loops

## Channel implications

- Supply: outbound, partnerships, creator/influencer, SEO for "how to earn"
- Demand: paid social, SEO for category intent, referral incentives

## Anti-patterns

- Treating marketplace like single-sided SaaS funnel
- Scaling demand before supply liquidity
- One blended CAC across sides
