# Audience Mapping

**Used by:** Funnel Architect (Phase 1 — Discovery, always first if audience unclear)

## ICP interview (prioritized — ask top 6 minimum)

1. Who buys vs. who uses? (economic buyer vs. champion)
2. Firmographics / demographics (size, industry, geo, budget band)
3. What triggered them to look for a solution now?
4. What were they using before (including "nothing" / spreadsheets)?
5. Top 3 objections to buying
6. What does success look like 90 days after purchase?

## JTBD capture

| Job type | Question |
|----------|----------|
| Functional | What task must get done? |
| Emotional | How do they want to feel? |
| Social | How do they want to be perceived? |

**Forces of progress:** push of situation, pull of new solution, anxiety of switch, habit of status quo.

## Persona template (evidence-based)

Avoid generic "Marketing Mary." Require:

- Role / segment label
- Trigger event (observed or from VoC)
- Success metric they care about
- Top objection
- Alternative considered

## Objection inventory

List top 5 reasons people don't buy. Tag: rational vs. emotional vs. procedural.

## Voice-of-customer sources

- G2/Capterra reviews (patterns, not single quotes)
- Support tickets / sales call themes (if user provides)
- Reddit / community threads (Tier C — triangulate)

## Output

Audience block for Funnel Spec brief recap (≤4 lines + objection table).
